# How to play retro games with me (RetroConnect)

Do these in order. Don't skip anything.
If your screen looks different from what's written here, **stop and text me**.

Before you start, text me: **"send me the invite"**. I'll send you a long
code that starts with `RC1-`. You'll need it in step 9.


## Step 1: Download it

1. Click this link:
   **https://github.com/phantomdenied/RetroConnect/releases/latest/download/RetroConnect-Setup.exe**

2. Wait for it to finish downloading. It's big (about 120 MB).


## Step 2: Install it (and the scary warning)

3. Open your **Downloads** folder. Double-click **RetroConnect-Setup**.

4. A **blue box** pops up that says **"Windows protected your PC"**.
   **This is normal. It's not a virus.** Windows says this about any app
   that isn't from a big company.
   - Click the small underlined words **More info**.
   - A **Run anyway** button shows up at the bottom. Click it.

5. Click through the installer (**Next**, **Install**, **Finish**). RetroConnect opens.
   (If it doesn't: press the Windows key, type **RetroConnect**, press Enter.)

6. A **firewall box** might pop up asking about RetroConnect.
   Click **Allow**. (If you click Cancel, we can't play together.)


## Step 3: The welcome screens

7. Type a nickname (anything). **Leave the ROM folder exactly as it is.**
   Click **Next**.
   (The app makes that folder for you. Games you get from me go in there
   and sort themselves. You never have to touch it.)

8. RetroArch screen: just click **Next**. (Step 4 below installs it.)

9. "Pair with your buddy" screen:
   - Click **My buddy sent me one**.
   - Click the box, then press **Ctrl+V** to paste the `RC1-` code I sent you.
   - It says **"✓ Invite code"**. Click **Finish**.

10. Now it connects us **by itself**. It installs a program called **Tailscale**.
    - Windows asks **"Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"**
      Click **Yes**.
    - Wait. When the bottom bar says it's done, we're connected.
    - You do **not** need to make an account or sign in to anything.


## Step 4: The "do everything" button

11. Plug in your controller.

12. Click **RetroArch** on the left side.

13. Click the big button **Set it all up for me**.
    Now **wait**. It downloads the game player and all the emulators.
    It can take 5 minutes or more. Don't close anything.
    It's done when it says **"All set!"**.

14. Press any button on your controller once (with the RetroConnect window
    in front) so it notices the controller.

15. Click **Test RetroArch**. A black window opens. In the corner it should
    say your controller is **"configured"**. Close that window.


## Step 5: Look through my games

16. On the left side there's a button with **my name** on it
    (it says "[my name]'s games"). Click it. That's every game I have.

17. Use the search box, or the drop-down filters at the top
    (System, Multiplayer, Genre and so on) to find something.

18. Click **Get it** on any game you want. It copies to your computer.
    (You don't *have* to. If I start a game you don't have, you get it
    automatically.)

19. **Games** on the left side shows the games on *your* computer.


## Step 6: Playing (every time from now on)

20. Open RetroConnect and leave it open.

21. When I start a game, a bar pops up at the top of the app.
    Click **Join game**. The game opens. That's it.

22. While playing:
    - **Start + Select** at the same time opens the game menu (to quit).
    - Taking turns on a 1-player game? Either of us clicks
      **Swap players** in the bar at the top of RetroConnect.


## Sending each other files

The **Files** page is for anything that isn't a game: save files, manuals, pictures, mods.

- **To send me something:** click **Add files** (or **Add folder**) and pick it.
  Leave RetroConnect open until I've got it.
- **When I send you something:** the Files button in the menu shows a number,
  like **Files (1)**. Open it and click **Download**. It goes into your
  **Downloads** folder, inside a folder called **RetroConnect**. Click **Show** to find it.

## Updates

23. When there's a new version, a bar shows up at the top of the app.
    Click **Download update**, wait a moment, then click **Restart to update**.
    RetroConnect closes and opens again by itself, updated. Your stuff stays.


## If something goes wrong

- Close RetroConnect and open it again. That fixes most things.
- Still broken? Take a screenshot (**Windows key + Shift + S**, then drag a box)
  and send it to me.
- Don't post the `RC1-` code anywhere. It's like a password.
